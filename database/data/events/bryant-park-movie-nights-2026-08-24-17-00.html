About the event

Welcome to the 33rd season of summer movies in Bryant Park! Bring a blanket and a friend, grab your spot on the lawn, and watch a feature film on the big screen under the iconic Manhattan skyline.

The beloved Monday night tradition is completely free to the public, featuring an incredible lineup of award-winning classics, fan favorites, and delicious local food vendors curated by the Hester Street Fair.

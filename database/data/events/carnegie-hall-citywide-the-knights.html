About the Event: Carnegie Hall Citywide: The Knights

Celebrate Fourth of July weekend and the lead-up to America's 250th anniversary with a spectacular, free outdoor performance by the intrepid chamber orchestra, The Knights. Described as a “fellowship of adventurous musicians,” this ensemble has earned a fervent following at Carnegie Hall and across New York City for their boundary-pushing programs.

Led by Colin and Eric Jacobsen, the orchestra brings an extraordinary repertoire that fuses classical music with diverse American roots and influences in true, innovative "Knightsian" fashion.

The program features highly anticipated selections, including:

  • Arrangements of Margaret Bonds’s “Troubled Water” (based on the spiritual “Wade in the Water”).

  • Selections from Samuel Coleridge-Taylor’s 24 Negro Melodies, brilliantly arranged by violinist Curtis Stewart.

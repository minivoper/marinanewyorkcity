About the event

This summer-long musical showcase brings elite local talent directly to the heart of Midtown Manhattan. Borrowing incredible acts from the 34th Street Partnership’s ‘Ensembles in Residence’ program, the series gives a variety of musicians the opportunity to bring their popular weekly pop-up performances to a grander stage on the Bryant Park Upper Terrace.

The diverse lineup features everything from premium party brass band Triad to legendary ragtime and jazz pianist Terry Waldo and his Gotham City Band. Visitors will also catch a stellar selection of up-and-coming local acts, including EW Harris, the Kyle Lacy Band, and Chris Q. Murphy and the Automat.

Event Details

  • Dates: Wednesdays throughout the summer season

  • Time: 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM

  • Location: The Upper Terrace at Bryant Park

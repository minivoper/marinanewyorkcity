About the event

The streets of New York City will light up with love, resilience, and unforgettable energy for the annual NYC Pride March. What began as a historic demonstration honoring the 1969 Stonewall Riots has evolved into a powerful global symbol of LGBTQIA+ unity, activism, and community.

Millions of spectators, allies, marching contingents, and game-changing Grand Marshals will come together in the heart of the city to move in defiant advocacy, solidarity, and celebration.

We also take reasonable steps to accommodate users from other jurisdictions, including the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA).

 

1. What Information We Collect

We collect two types of information from visitors and users of the Website:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Account registration: name, email address, and password when you create a user account;

  • Comments and likes: any content you submit when commenting on articles or engaging with community features;

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address if you subscribe to updates or promotional communications;

  • User profile information: any optional profile details you choose to add to your account.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate geographic location;

  • Browser type, operating system, and device type;

  • Pages visited, time spent on each page, and navigation paths;

  • Referring URLs (the page you came from before visiting our site);

  • Session information such as page response times and browsing behavior;

  • Unique device identifiers and cookie data.

 

2. How We Collect Your Information

We collect your personal information through the following means:

  1. Directly from you: when you register an account, post a comment, like an article, submit a contact form, or sign up for communications.

  2. Automatically: through cookies, web beacons, and analytics tools when you browse the Website.

  3. From third-party services: analytics providers (such as Google Analytics), advertising networks, and affiliate tracking platforms may share aggregated or individual data with us as described below.

  4. From affiliate networks: when you click an affiliate link on our Website and complete a qualifying action, affiliate platforms may share transaction data with us for commission tracking purposes.

We collect and use your personal information for the following purposes:

  1. To operate and maintain the Website, including publishing news content and managing user accounts;

  2. To enable community features such as commenting on and liking articles;

  3. To respond to your service inquiries and contact form submissions;

  4. To send you newsletters, promotional content, and updates about the Website (with your consent, and subject to your opt-out rights);

  5. To track affiliate link activity and calculate any applicable commissions;

  6. To understand how visitors use the Website through analytics, so we can improve content and user experience;

  7. To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse of the Website;

  8. To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations;

  9. To enforce our Terms and Conditions.

 

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

The Website is hosted on the Wix.com platform. Your data may be stored through Wix's data storage systems, databases, and applications on secure servers protected by industry-standard safeguards, including firewalls and encryption.

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

Affiliate links on this Website may use tracking cookies or pixels operated by third-party affiliate networks. 6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and support affiliate link tracking. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your web browser.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: necessary for the basic operation of the Website, such as maintaining your login session;

  • Analytics cookies: used to collect information about how visitors use the Website (e.g., Google Analytics). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally;

  • Preference cookies: used to remember your settings and preferences;

  • Affiliate and advertising cookies: used by third-party affiliate networks to track clicks and referrals from our Website.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Website. For more information about managing cookies, visit your browser's help documentation.

Third-party services embedded on our Website, such as Google Analytics, may also use their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

California residents may also have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

EU and UK residents may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the contact form at marinanewyorkcity.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

Morning ☀️

Start your day in Central Park with a coffee and a walk through the park.Then head down Fifth Avenue for iconic NYC views, luxury stores, and landmarks like St. Patrick's Cathedral.

Midday 🏙️

Visit Top of the Rock or SUMMIT One Vanderbilt for breathtaking skyline views.After that, walk through Grand Central Terminal and explore nearby Midtown streets.

Afternoon 🍕

Grab a classic New York pizza slice, then head downtown to Brooklyn Bridge for one of the best walks in the city.Explore DUMBO for waterfront views, cobblestone streets, and skyline photo spots.

Evening 🌆

Watch sunset from Empire State Building or along the waterfront at Brooklyn Bridge Park.End the night in Times Square when the city lights come alive.



New York is impossible to fully see in one day — but this route gives you a little bit of the city’s magic.

Summer evenings in New York City offer a unique chance to enjoy music outdoors, and this year, Abrons Arts Center invites everyone to experience live jazz in a welcoming community setting. The newly renovated Miriam and Harold Steinberg Plaza on the Lower East Side will come alive with vibrant performances during the Summer in the Plaza series. This free event promises memorable nights filled with soulful sounds and local artistry on July 17 and August 15, 2026.


A New Venue for Community and Music


Abrons Arts Center has long been a cultural hub in the Lower East Side, and the recent renovation of the Miriam and Harold Steinberg Plaza enhances its role as a gathering place. The open-air plaza offers an inviting atmosphere where neighbors and visitors can relax on warm summer nights while enjoying live music. The design improvements create a comfortable space that supports both performers and audiences, making it ideal for the free jazz series.


The plaza’s transformation reflects a commitment to community engagement through the arts. It encourages people to come together, share experiences, and connect through music. This year’s series builds on that vision by featuring standout local artists who bring fresh energy and creativity to the stage.


Meet the Featured Artist: Zacchae’us Paul


At the heart of the Summer in the Plaza series is multi-instrumentalist singer-songwriter Zacchae’us Paul. Known for his rich blend of styles, Paul’s music combines jazz with elements of soul, hip-hop, and spoken word. His work explores important social themes, including historical reparations and the strength found in black culture and spirituality.


Paul’s performances are more than concerts; they are conversations with the community. His ability to weave together different musical threads creates a dynamic experience that resonates deeply with listeners. For both dates in July and August, Paul will lead his band in delivering performances that highlight the power of music rooted in local neighborhoods.


Supporting Artists and Additional Attractions


The July 17 event will feature lyricist Tris10 and vocalist-composer Vanessa Camacho alongside Zacchae’us Paul and his band. These artists bring their own unique voices and styles, enriching the evening with diverse sounds and perspectives. The lineup for the August 15 show will be announced soon, promising more exciting collaborations.


In addition to the music, visitors can explore magic ephemera, a group exhibition running through August 2. This show features works by the 2024-26 New York Community Trust Van Lier Fellows and their mentors, offering a visual complement to the musical performances. The exhibition adds another layer of cultural engagement, making the plaza a vibrant arts destination throughout the summer.


What to Expect at the Events


  • Dates and Times: Friday, July 17 and Saturday, August 15, from 6 to 8 PM

  • Location: Miriam and Harold Steinberg Plaza, Abrons Arts Center, Lower East Side, New York City

  • Admission: Free, with RSVP available HERE

  • Atmosphere: Casual and family-friendly, perfect for gathering with friends or enjoying a solo evening of music

  • Additional Features: Art exhibition on view through August 2


Attendees are encouraged to bring blankets or lawn chairs for seating, although some seating will be provided. The plaza’s layout allows for easy social distancing and comfortable viewing of the performances.


Why Attend the Summer in the Plaza Series


This series offers a rare chance to enjoy high-quality live jazz without the usual cost or formality of indoor venues. It supports local artists and fosters a sense of community through shared cultural experiences. For jazz lovers and newcomers alike, the events provide an accessible way to discover new music and connect with the vibrant Lower East Side arts scene.


Zacchae’us Paul’s music, in particular, offers thoughtful reflections on society and identity, making the performances both entertaining and meaningful. The combination of live music and visual art creates a rich sensory experience that highlights the creativity thriving in New York City.


How to Prepare for Your Visit


  • RSVP in advance to secure your spot, as space is limited.

  • Arrive early to find good seating and explore the plaza.

  • Check the weather forecast and dress accordingly, since the event is outdoors.

  • Bring water and light snacks if desired, though nearby food options are available.

  • Plan to stay for the full two hours to enjoy the complete lineup and atmosphere.


The plaza is accessible by public transportation, making it easy to reach from anywhere in the city.


Supporting Local Arts Through Free Events


Abrons Arts Center’s Summer in the Plaza series demonstrates how free cultural events can enrich urban life. By providing a platform for artists like Zacchae’us Paul and others, the center helps sustain the creative community and invites everyone to participate. These events also highlight the importance of public spaces that encourage artistic expression and social connection.


The inclusion of the magic ephemera exhibition further supports emerging artists and fosters dialogue between different art forms. This holistic approach to community arts programming benefits both creators and audiences.

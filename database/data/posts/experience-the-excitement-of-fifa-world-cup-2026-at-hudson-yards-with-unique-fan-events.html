Fly Your Flag at Vessel

One of the standout fan events at Hudson Yards is Fly Your Flag at Vessel, presented by American Airlines. This interactive experience invites fans to explore the iconic Vessel structure while celebrating the diversity of the World Cup.


How to participate:


  1. Purchase your Fly Your Flag ticket.

  2. Search for six different countries’ flags hidden throughout Vessel.

  3. Stamp your card at each flag location.

  4. Redeem your fully stamped card for exclusive rewards at Hudson Yards.


This event encourages fans to engage with the tournament in a fun, active way while exploring one of New York’s most unique landmarks. The rewards include discounts, merchandise, and special offers that enhance your World Cup experience.


Spot The Bag for a Chance to Win


American Airlines brings an exciting twist to the World Cup festivities with the Spot The Bag challenge. This event takes the excitement 100 stories high at Edge NYC, offering fans a chance to win tickets to the exclusive Final Party.


How it works:


  • Look for the large suitcase near Vessel.

  • Scan QR codes placed around Hudson Yards and Edge NYC.

  • Engage with interactive content related to the tournament.

  • Enter for a chance to win a once-in-a-lifetime experience at the American Airlines x FIFA World Cup 26™ Final Party at Edge NYC.


This event connects the streets of the host cities with the breathtaking views from one of New York’s highest outdoor observation decks, making it a memorable part of your World Cup journey.

The Art of The Game Public Arts Program

Adding a cultural dimension to the World Cup celebrations, the Art of The Game program showcases 23 mega-balls across New York City and New Jersey. These large sculptures feature artwork from distinguished artists inspired by soccer and the spirit of the tournament.


At Hudson Yards, you can see some of these installations up close, blending sports and art in a way that highlights the global nature of the World Cup. This public art program invites fans to appreciate creativity alongside athleticism.
